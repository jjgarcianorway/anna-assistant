[2m│ Sampling rate[0m
[2m│ The sampling rate determines how frequently the governor checks to tune the CPU. sampling_down_factor is a tunable that multiplies the sampling rate when the CPU is at its highest clock frequency, thereby delaying load evaluation and improving performance. Allowed values for sampling_down_factor are 1 to 100000.  This tunable has no effect on behavior at lower CPU frequencies/loads.[0m
[2m│ [0m
[2m│ To read the value (default = 1), run:[0m
[2m│ [0m
